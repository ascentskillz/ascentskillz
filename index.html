About the tutor

I started tutoring in 11th grade, helping friends and classmates who were stuck in higher-level math courses — that's grown into what Ascent is today. In high school I passed the AMC 12, which qualified me for the AIME, and I went on to major in Aerospace Engineering.

My approach is built around solving problems, not just reviewing concepts. Understanding an idea and being able to execute it under exam pressure are two different skills, so sessions lean heavy on practice. I push students hard and I can be blunt about it, but it comes from wanting them to get genuinely competitive — and, eventually, to actually like math.

Outside of the material, I'm talkative and easy to work with, and every student gets the same effort from me regardless of where they're starting. I look forward to meeting a new group of students each year.
